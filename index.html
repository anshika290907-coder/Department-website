About the department

The department of Political Science at Sri Venkateswara College, University Of Delhi , provides students with and understanding of political institutions , theories and governance.

The department encourages critical thinking, academic discussions, research and participation in various activities.

courses offered

Undergraduate

the department offers undergraduate course in political science , focusing on political theory, indian government and comparative politics.

Postgraduate

Postgraduate studies provide advanced knowledge of political institutions, political thought, and public administration.

Seminars

The department organizes seminars and academic discussions to encourage students to explore important political issues.

Events

The department conducts various academic events, workshops and student activities throughout the year.
